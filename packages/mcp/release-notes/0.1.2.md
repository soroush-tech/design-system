## @soroush.design/mcp@0.1.2

The bundled documentation is regenerated from the current repo, with no change to the tools. This
is the first release made by the publishing workflow, through npm trusted publishing.

### Changed

- `get_doc`, `get_component`, `list_components` and `search_docs` describe the components added
  to the design system since 0.1.1: `NavTree`, `NavGroup` and `NavLink`; `Sidebar` as a navigation
  rail (`layout="stacked"`, `stackedWidth` and `panel`); a `tonal` `Tabs` variant; and nine new
  icons.
- The six tools, their inputs and their output are unchanged.
