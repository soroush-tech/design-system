## @soroush.tech/mcp@0.1.1

Serves the design system 2.0 documentation, and a code-quality fix with no change to the tools.
([#7](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/pull/7))

### Changed

- The bundled content is regenerated from the current repo, so `get_doc`, `get_component` and
  `search_docs` describe design-system 2.0 and styled-system 6.0: the frame-only `layout` prop,
  columns on `Grid` with `gap` as the gutter, `span="full"`, `LayoutProvider`, the "Layout: one
  way per intent" table, and `installPopover()` on `@soroush.tech/design-system/testing`.
- The tool handlers are plain functions rather than `async` ones that awaited nothing. The six
  tools, their inputs and their output are unchanged.
