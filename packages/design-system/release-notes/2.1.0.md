## @soroush.tech/design-system@2.1.0

`Tooltip` takes a disabled control, a description that holds while it is closed, and a point to be
placed at, all asked for by the design studio when it moved off the native `title`.

### Added

- **`Tooltip` `isDescriptionPersistent`** - with `shouldDescribeChild`, the child is described the
  whole time, as by a native `title`, not only while the tooltip is open: a visually hidden copy of
  the `title` is rendered beside the child for `aria-describedby` to point at. It is also a theme
  default (`theme.components.Tooltip.defaultProps`).
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/blob/main/packages/design-system/src/Tooltip/README.md)

- **`Tooltip` `anchorPosition`** - a point in the window (client coordinates, as Popover's) to place
  the tooltip at, for a hint that depends on where the pointer is. `children` is optional then, and
  `isOpen` opens it; it keeps its gap from the point and flips for want of room.
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/blob/main/packages/design-system/src/Tooltip/README.md)

- **`Tooltip` `isInteractive`** - with `false` the tooltip takes no pointer events, so a click goes to
  what is under it, and it closes as the pointer leaves the child: for a text hint that holds nothing to
  use. The default (`true`) keeps it hoverable (WCAG 1.4.13). Also a theme default.
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/blob/main/packages/design-system/src/Tooltip/README.md)

- **`Tooltip` hands what it is handed to its child** - a parent that clones its child (a `Menu` with a
  `Tooltip` for its trigger) now reaches the button: the `ref`, `id`, `aria-*` and handlers go on to
  it, the child's own handler and the handed one both run, and a handed description is added to. A child
  hidden from assistive technology is not named by the title. `shouldWrapDisabledChild` (default `true`,
  also a theme default) leaves out the wrapper of a disabled child that still takes the pointer.
- **`TooltipGroup`** - tooltips inside it open with no enter delay while another is open, or within
  `skipDelayDuration` (300 ms) of the last one closing, so a toolbar reads icon to icon; the one still
  showing closes at once. Tooltips outside a group, touch and controlled tooltips (`isOpen`) are
  unchanged. `skipDelayDuration` in `theme.components.Tooltip.defaultProps` does the same for every
  tooltip under a theme, with no wrapper (unset or 0 is off; a `TooltipGroup` wins over it).
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/blob/main/packages/design-system/src/Tooltip/README.md)
- **`ContextMenu` opens from code** - `isOpen` and `anchorPosition` (client coordinates, as Popover's)
  open it at a point whatever was right-clicked, with `target` optional; it asks to close through
  `onClose`, moves as the point does, and gives the focus back to where it was.
- **`Menu` and `ContextMenu` `slotProps.list`** - `data-*`, class and `aria-*` for the `role="menu"`
  list, and for every submenu's; an `onKeyDown` runs before the list's own. Its `role`, `id` and name stay
  the menu's.

### Fixed

- **A press of a `Tooltip`'s child closes it** - as a native `title` goes away, so an open tooltip is no
  longer left over what the pointer goes on to click. A touch is left to its long press.
- **`Tooltip` over a disabled child** - a disabled control gets no pointer events from React, so the
  tooltip never opened. It now wraps a disabled child in an inline-grid `span` and listens and is
  placed on that; the child keeps its own `ref`.
- **No native `title` in `Tabs` and `MenuItem`** - the close and overflow buttons of `Tabs` show their
  labels, and a `MenuItem`'s `note`, as non-interactive tooltips; the note still describes the row, open
  or closed, and opens over a disabled row.
