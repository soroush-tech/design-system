## @soroush.tech/markdown@1.2.3

Works with `@soroush.tech/design-system` 2: the peer range covers it, and the toolbar's dropdowns
are design-system `Select`s. ([#9](https://github.com/soroush-tech/design-system/issues/9))

### Changed

- The `Toolbar`'s heading, code-language and diagram dropdowns are `Select`s on the native path
  (`<Select native>`) instead of `NativeSelect`, following the design system's "always `Select`"
  guidance. They render the same native `<select>`, keep their accessible names
  (`Heading level`, `Code block language`, `Mermaid diagram type`) and still reset to their
  placeholder after each pick.

### Packaging

- The `@soroush.tech/design-system` peer range is `^1.3.3 || ^2.0.0-alpha.0` (was `^1.3.3`), so
  design-system 2 consumers - its prereleases included - no longer get an unmet-peer warning.
  The range is now written out instead of derived from the workspace at publish time.
