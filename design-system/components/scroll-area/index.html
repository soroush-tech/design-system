ScrollArea

A scrolling area whose scrollbars are drawn over its content rather than beside it - so a rounded panel keeps its corner clean - and show only while the pointer is over the area or focus is inside it. Scrolling itself stays native: the wheel, the keyboard and touch work as they do anywhere. Each axis that overflows gets a track, down the right edge or along the bottom, whose thumb drags and whose track pages one view toward the pointer, as a native one does.

import { ScrollArea } from '@soroush.tech/design-system/ScrollArea'

;<ScrollArea maxHeight={240} aria-label="Notes" tabIndex={0}>
  {notes}
</ScrollArea>
